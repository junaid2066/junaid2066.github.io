I am an AI Researcher at the Natural Language Processing Lab of the Artificial Intelligence Technology Centre (AITeC), Islamabad. I lead the development of deep learning–based models and algorithms, and work with faculty and research teams to turn research opportunities into real-world AI solutions.

I completed my MS in Data Science (by research) at the University of Central Punjab, Lahore, after a Postgraduate Diploma in Mechatronics and Control Engineering at UET Lahore and a B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering at UCP. Before research, I spent more than eight years in R&D at AMBER Capacitors Limited, rising from Assistant Engineer to Deputy Manager R&D.

My graduate work covers computer vision, machine learning and deep learning, with a focus on crowd scene analysis. I have also worked on waste, rice, crop and medical image classification.
